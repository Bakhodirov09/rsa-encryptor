How this works

1
You paste your secret here. It never leaves your browser in readable form — encryption happens locally using JavaScript.
2
The secret is encrypted with the recipient's Public Key. Only their corresponding Private Key can decrypt it — mathematically guaranteed.
3
You copy the encrypted blob and send it through any channel — email, Slack, company messenger — it doesn't matter who reads it.
4
The recipient decrypts it privately on their machine. No password exchange needed.
🔑 🔒 📨
Zero server involvement. This page contains only the recipient's public key (which is meant to be public). Your plaintext is never transmitted anywhere. You can verify this by inspecting the network tab in DevTools — you'll see zero outgoing requests when you click Encrypt.
